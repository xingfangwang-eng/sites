Frequently Asked Questions

            
            

                
What is Agent KYA?

                
KYA (Know Your Agent) is a verification framework for decentralized agents, ensuring identity integrity for WebMCP and payment protocols.

            

            
            

                
What is a Verifiable Credential?

                
A verifiable credential is a tamper-evident credential with cryptographic proof of authenticity, enabling trustless verification on-chain.

            

            
            

                
What is WebMCP?

                
WebMCP (Web Monetization Control Protocol) is a standard for enabling microtransactions and payment streams on the web.

            

            
            

                
How is the credential hash generated?

                
The credential hash is generated using SHA-256 from the agent's DID, public key, and expiry date, ensuring integrity and verifiability.

            

            
            

                
Is this tool secure?

                
All processing happens in your browser. No data is sent to any server. Your keys and credentials never leave your device.

            

            
            

                
What formats are supported?

                
We support JSON format compatible with WebMCP and payment protocols. Export credentials for immediate integration.
