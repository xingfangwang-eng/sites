AI Safety Documentation

What is AI Hallucination in CoT?

AI hallucination refers to situations where a language model generates factually incorrect or misleading information while appearing confident. In Chain of Thought (CoT) reasoning, hallucinations can lead to flawed decision-making and unreliable outputs.

How to prevent Agent Boundary Violations?

Boundary violations occur when AI agents attempt to bypass system prompts, ignore instructions, or access restricted resources. Prevention involves implementing robust guardrails, monitoring reasoning patterns, and using tools like TraceGuard to detect violation attempts.

Glossary

Chain of Thought (CoT)
A reasoning process where an AI breaks down complex problems into sequential steps to arrive at a conclusion.
Hallucination
False or unsubstantiated claims generated by AI without factual basis.
Prompt Injection
An attack technique where malicious inputs override or manipulate an AI's instructions.
Boundary Violation
When an AI attempts to exceed its designated operational limits or security constraints.
