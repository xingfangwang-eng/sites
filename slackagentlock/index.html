Frequently Asked Questions

What is SlackAgent Lock?

SlackAgent Lock is a free online workflow template generator that helps you create payment locks, behavior locks, and hybrid locks for Slack and Agentforce platforms. No coding required — generate templates instantly and copy directly into your workflow builders.

What is a Payment Lock Workflow?

A Payment Lock restricts access to Slack channels or Agentforce features until payment is verified. Use cases include: paid membership communities, premium content channels, subscription services, and one-time purchase access. Automates revenue protection without manual intervention.

What is a Behavior Lock Workflow?

A Behavior Lock requires users to complete specific actions before gaining access. Common triggers include: joining a channel, reacting to a message, completing a survey, verifying email, or accepting terms. Perfect for engagement-based access and lead qualification workflows.

What is a Hybrid Lock Workflow?

A Hybrid Lock combines both payment and behavior requirements. Users must complete both payment verification AND specified actions to unlock content. Best for high-value services like premium paid courses with onboarding requirements, or enterprise accounts needing multiple verification steps.

How do I use the generated template?

Simple 3-step process: 1) Select your lock type and platform, 2) Enter trigger condition and optional rules, 3) Click Generate. Copy the template with one click and paste directly into Slack Workflow Builder or Agentforce Flow Builder. Ready to use immediately without modifications.

Is SlackAgent Lock free to use?

Yes, 100% free forever with no registration required. All template generation happens locally in your browser — no data is stored, transmitted, or saved. Zero cookies, zero tracking, zero account needed.

What platforms are supported?

SlackAgent Lock supports: Slack Workflow Builder (for Slack channels and workspace automation), Agentforce Automation (for AI-powered workflows), and Salesforce Flow (for traditional Salesforce automation). Templates can be generated for single platform or both simultaneously.

How do I copy and use the template?

Click the green "Copy Template to Clipboard" button after generation. For Slack: open Slack Workflow Builder → Create New Workflow → paste template. For Agentforce: open Flow Builder → New Flow → paste template. No formatting or modifications needed.
