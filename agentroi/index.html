The Economics of Agentic AI

Agentic AI represents a fundamental shift in how businesses approach repetitive knowledge work. By automating routine tasks while maintaining human oversight, organizations can achieve significant cost savings and efficiency gains.

Calculating AI Efficiency Multipliers

The efficiency multiplier is derived by comparing the total time required for manual task completion versus the combined AI processing time and human review time. This metric quantifies how much faster an agentic workflow can operate compared to traditional manual processes.
