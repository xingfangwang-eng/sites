Knowledge Base

Q: What are AI Nano-payments?

A: Micro-transactions between autonomous agents for API usage or computational resources.

Q: How does the Authorization Proof work?

A: It simulates the digital signature required for an agent to prove it has been granted a specific budget by its owner.

Q: Why use a payment sandbox?

A: To prevent "Runaway Agent Spend" by testing budget rules before deploying to a live blockchain or credit system.

Q: Is data stored?

A: No. AgentPay is 100% client-side; your rules and budget data never leave your browser.
