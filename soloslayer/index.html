Deep Dive FAQ

Why is context switching so expensive for solo founders?

For Indie Hackers and Solo Entrepreneurs, context switching is particularly costly due to the "brain cache flush" phenomenon. When you switch from coding to marketing, your prefrontal cortex must completely reconfigure its neural pathways. Research shows it takes an average of 25 minutes to fully re-enter a state of Deep Work after an interruption. This means each role transition creates significant Context Switching Debt that accumulates throughout your workday, eroding productivity by 20-40% according to cognitive science studies.

How does SoloSlayer calculate "Hidden Costs"?

SoloSlayer calculates Hidden Costs through a multi-factor algorithm: (1) Switch Timer - tracks the duration between initiating a role change and completing emotional offloading; (2) Offloading Quality - evaluates text length and complexity to measure mental release effectiveness; (3) Role Distance - assigns higher costs when switching between dissimilar roles (e.g., Developer to Marketing); (4) Context Debt Accumulation - compounds costs for rapid successive switches. This gives Solo Entrepreneurs a precise metric for the real cost of role transitions.

How to effectively use SoloSlayer for role management?

To maximize effectiveness, Indie Hackers should: (1) Set A-Priority Tasks - Always define 1-2 critical tasks per role daily; (2) Enforce Offloading - Never skip the emotional offloading step, even for short switches; (3) Monitor Context Debt - Keep it below 30 minutes to maintain Deep Work capacity; (4) Batch Similar Tasks - Group related activities to minimize transitions; (5) Review Daily Reports - Analyze role distribution to optimize future planning. Consistency is key - treat role switching like a disciplined Solo Entrepreneur would treat any critical business process.
